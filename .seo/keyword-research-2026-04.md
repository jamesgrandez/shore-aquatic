# Shore Aquatic — Keyword Research (AnswerThePublic, Apr 2026)

## 1. Aquarium plants — biggest opportunity

### Pain-point / troubleshooting queries (high intent, high search volume)
- why are aquarium plants turning **brown / yellow / black**
- why are aquarium plants **melting / dying**
- why my aquarium plants are melting
- will aquarium plants grow back after melting
- why is it important to have plants in the aquarium

### How-to cluster (top of funnel)
- **how to care for aquarium plants** (broad head)

- how to keep aquarium plants alive
- how to grow live aquarium plants
- how to plant aquarium plants in gravel
- how to grow aquarium plants from seed
- how to clean plastic / fake aquarium plants

- is co2 necessary for aquarium plants
- will aquarium plants grow with / under led lights
- will aquarium plants grow under blue light

### Fish-compatibility cluster (commercial intent)
- best aquarium plants for **bettas / shrimp / goldfish / guppies**

### Beginner / easy cluster
- best beginner aquarium plants
- beginner-friendly live aquarium plants

## 3. Water lilies — HIGH noise from Monet; real buyer intent is narrower

### ⚠️ Noise to ignore
Significant chunk of queries is about **Claude Monet paintings, movies, museums, print prices** — these have zero commercial value for us. Filter them out.

### Real gardening intent
- **water lilies vs lotus** (7+ comparison queries — major confusion point)
- are water lilies and lotus the same / are water lilies lotuses
- hardy vs tropical water lilies
- how to grow water lilies (from seed / in a bowl / indoors)
- how to plant water lilies
- can water lilies grow **indoors / in shade / without soil / in shallow water / in fish tank**
- do water lilies need full sun / soil
- do water lilies come back every year
- will water lilies take over a pond / survive winter / spread
- how often to water lilies (watering frequency confusion)

### Safety queries (feature this in care guides + FAQ)
- are water lilies poisonous (to **cats / dogs / humans / koi**)
- are water lilies edible / which water lilies are edible
- are water lilies safe for cats

### Commercial intent
- water lilies for sale / near me / online
- hardy water lilies for sale
- tropical water lilies for sale
- miniature water lilies near me
- best soil for water lilies
